## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Mission
The design system is architected for institutional clinical governance, mission-critical workforce scheduling, and operational harmony within inpatient eldercare and convalescent hospital environments. The interface balances high-stakes precision with reassuring composure:
- **Clinical Rigor & Legality**: Eliminates scheduling errors, legal violations, and staffing shortfalls through unambiguous visual feedback and uncompromising layout discipline.
- **Cognitive Clarity Under Pressure**: Reduces visual fatigue for head nurses, directors of nursing, and care team leaders who spend hours reviewing high-density multi-ward rosters.
- **Inclusive Accessibility**: Provides warm, immediate affordances designed for diverse workforce demographics, ranging from hospital executives on desktop monitors to senior care workers reviewing shifts on mobile screens.

### Design Movement: Modern Functional Healthcare Minimal
Drawing from high-density data visualization and human-centered clinical systems, this design system combines:
- **Clean Healthcare Minimalism**: Crisp white, warm neutral surfaces, and clinical deep teal anchor structures, eliminating non-functional embellishments.
- **Precision Data Grid Semantics**: A compact, high-contrast shift-token system where distinct color codings instantly convey day, evening, night, and off statuses without ambiguity.
- **Tactile State Guidance**: Subtle tonal elevation and definitive borders rather than heavy blur effects ensure instant scanning on hospital-grade monitors and mobile devices.

## Colors

### Palette Strategy
The primary foundation is **Deep Clinical Teal (`#0D5C63`)**, evoking surgical precision, stability, and managerial authority. Paired with **Clinical Slate Blue (`#1976D2`)** for transactional actions and **Vital Emerald (`#2A9D8F`)** for healthy staffing compliance, the chromatic baseline projects trust and administrative calm.

### Semantic Roster Color Tokens
The monthly roster grid operates on a calibrated color coding system that meets WCAG 2.1 AA contrast requirements against bright tabular backgrounds:
- **D (Day / 주간)**: Sunrise Amber (`#FEF3C7` bg / `#92400E` text / `#FCD34D` border). Denotes active morning and afternoon rounds.
- **E (Evening / 이브닝)**: Warm Dusk Orange (`#FFEDD5` bg / `#9A3412` text / `#FDBA74` border). Signifies transition and sundown care hours.
- **N (Night / 나이트)**: Deep Indigo Night (`#EEF2FF` bg / `#312E81` text / `#C7D2FE` border). Instantly isolates high-risk consecutive night shifts.
- **O (Off / 휴무·비번)**: Subdued Slate Grey (`#F1F5F9` bg / `#475569` text / `#CBD5E1` border). Visually recedes to let working shifts stand out.
- **Y / R (Annual & Requested Leave / 연차·희망휴무)**: Mint Emerald (`#ECFDF5` bg / `#065F46` text / `#A7F3D0` border). Highlights personal time-off allocations.

### Rule Violation & Compliance States
- **Violation (Error/Illegal)**: Vivid Crimson (`#FEF2F2` bg / `#991B1B` text / `#EF4444` border). Applied to statutory limits (11-hour rest deficit, weekly hour caps, minimum head count failures). Blocks final roster lock.
- **Warning (Notice/Soft Rule)**: Warm Amber (`#FFFBEB` bg / `#854D0E` text / `#F59E0B` border). Signals operational targets (excess consecutive workdays, unassigned veteran staff). Allows conditional override with logged audit reasoning.

## Typography

### Structural Pairings
- **Headings & Structural Titles**: **Manrope** lends geometric balance, architectural solidity, and clean executive presence to hospital administrative dashboards and ward headers.
- **Data Tables & Shift Grids**: **Inter** is chosen for tabular numerical clarity, distinguished letterforms (e.g., '1', 'I', 'l'), and zero distortion at compact sizes (`10px` to `13px`).

### Tabular Typography Rules
- All numeric counts across the grid (daily staff tallies, accumulated night shift totals, overtime tallies) must use **tabular figures** (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal jitter during real-time solver recalculations.
- Shift code letters (`D`, `E`, `N`, `O`, `Y`) inside table cells are rendered in `cell-shift-code` (`13px / 700 / uppercase`) with centered baseline alignment for instant scannability.
- On the mobile caregiver app, shifts utilize `mobile-hero-shift` to ensure that elderly caregivers and attendants can immediately read their assignment without pinching or zooming.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Model: The High-Density Fluid Roster Canvas
The platform prioritizes a **data-dense, horizontal layout model** tailored to standard 1920×1080 and 1440×900 desktop workstations used in nursing stations:
- **Master Roster Screen (Desktop)**: Split into three synced zones:
  1. *Left Sticky Axis* (width: `240px`): Ward staff meta cards (name, tenure tier, shift balance indicator, night-allowance tag).
  2. *Central Fluid Roster Canvas*: 28–31 day columns. Minimum cell width is `36px`, dynamically expanding to fill horizontal viewport width while maintaining sticky day-of-week headers.
  3. *Right Collapsible Inspector* (width: `320px`): Real-time constraint violations, solver recommendations, and individual shift swap validations.
  4. *Bottom Sticky Aggregate Row*: Real-time D/E/N staffing totals per day compared directly against mandatory ward thresholds.

### Roster Grid Rhythm
- **Cell Padding**: Strict `space-2xs` (2px) vertical and `space-xs` (4px) horizontal padding to maximize data density while preserving distinct clickable bounding boxes.
- **Rhythm Anchors**:
  - `space-xs` (4px) between badges and status dots.
  - `space-sm` (8px) between component inputs and toolbars.
  - `space-lg` (16px) section padding for inspector drawers and modal cards.
  - `margin` (24px) outer dashboard boundary.

### Mobile Form Factor Reflow
For care staff checking schedules on personal smartphones:
- Single-column card agenda view replaces the full matrix.
- Large date headers with full-width shift status banners (`min-height: 56px`) allow simple thumb tapping.
- Action drawer for shift swap requests anchors to the bottom with safe-area spacing.

## Elevation & Depth

### Depth Philosophy: Low-Elevation Surface Hierarchy
In a dense data spreadsheet environment, ambient drop shadows cause visual noise and obscure fine table borders. The design system uses crisp border definitions, subtle tone stacking, and tactical surface lifts.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas - `#F8FAFC`)**: The operational backdrop hosting non-editable table borders and workspace backgrounds.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Sheets & Modals - `#FFFFFF`)**: The active grid cells, toolbar containers, and inspector cards. Distinguished by crisp `#E2E8F0` hairline dividers.
- **Tier 2 (Sticky Headers & Frozen Columns)**: Header rows and sticky staff name columns hover over scrolling grid planes with a 1px border (`#CBD5E1`) and a micro edge occlusion (`0 2px 4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`).
- **Tier 3 (Active Draggable Shift Tokens & Flyovers)**: When a cell is dragged or an inspection popover is opened, the element lifts cleanly: `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(13, 92, 99, 0.12), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`.
- **Tier 4 (Validation Drawers & Alert Toasts)**: Positioned above all grid content with `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

### Shape Language: Soft Precision
A compact roundedness level (`roundedness: 1` — 4px base radius) preserves structural geometry inside the dense roster grid.
- **Shift Badges & Cells**: `rounded` (4px / `0.25rem`) provides clean separation without turning cells into bloated bubbles, preserving maximum legible area.
- **Form Controls & Inputs**: `rounded` (4px) for rapid keyboard tab-indexing and compact alignment.
- **Cards, Drawers & System Modals**: `rounded-lg` (8px / `0.5rem`) adds polite polish to floating overlays.
- **Status Indicator Dots & Avatars**: Circular (`9999px`) for quick human recognition of staff profiles.

## Components

### Roster Grid Cell
- **Dimensions**: Desktop default `36px` to `48px` wide, `40px` high.
- **States**:
  - *Default Filled*: Distinct shift token background (e.g., `#FEF3C7` for Day) with inner `1px` subtle outline (`#FCD34D`). Shift code centered.
  - *Previous Month Spillover*: `opacity: 0.4` with dashed border indicating reference-only shifts.
  - *Active Focused Cell*: `2px` solid `#0D5C63` outline with `2px` focus offset. Allows direct keyboard character overwrite (`D`, `E`, `N`, `O`, `Y`).
  - *Violation State*: Bottom-right triangle marker in Crimson `#EF4444` or double ring for severe statutory breaches.
  - *Hover State*: Light overlay with quick-tooltip displaying exact hours (e.g., `07:00 ~ 15:00`).

### Shift Pill Badges (Chips)
- Compact chip displaying Shift Code and optional headcount count.
- Padding: `2px 6px`. Height: `22px`.
- Typography: `cell-shift-code` (`13px`, semi-bold/bold).
- Variants mapped directly to `shift-*` tokens.

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Auto-Generate, Lock Roster)**: Deep Clinical Teal background (`#0D5C63`), text `#FFFFFF`, hover `#0A474D`. Height: `36px` (desktop), `48px` (mobile touch target).
- **Secondary (Compare Drafts, Export Excel)**: `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px` `#CBD5E1` border, text `#334155`, hover `#F8FAFC`.
- **Destructive/Violation Action**: Crimson `#EF4444` background with white text, or ghost button with `#991B1B` text.

### Constraint Inspector Panel (Side Drawer)
- Fixed right panel listing automated rule evaluations.
- **Violations Grouping**:
  - *Statutory Violations (Red)*: Red badge with icon, law citation (e.g., "근로기준법 제59조: 11시간 연속 휴식 미달"), affected staff name, and a "Jump to Cell" button.
  - *Operational Warnings (Yellow)*: Amber tag, hospital guideline deviation note (e.g., "나이트 3연속 초과"), and quick-resolve recommendation chip.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Standard height: `32px` in data density modes, `40px` in setup wizards.
- Border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`, focusing to `1.5px solid #0D5C63` with soft teal focus halo (`rgba(13, 92, 99, 0.15)`).

### Mobile Caregiver Quick Card
- Designed specifically for phone screens.
- Displays Today's Shift prominently: Date, large badge (e.g., `DAY 주간`), exact work hours (`07:00 ~ 15:00`), assigned ward, and partner staff on duty.
- Bottom actions: "근무 교환 요청 (Request Swap)" and "희망 휴무 신청 (Request Leave)" within thumb's reach.
